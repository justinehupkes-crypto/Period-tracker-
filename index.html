Is it hormonal?

Each symptom is matched against the cycle phase you were in when you logged it. Symptoms that keep landing in the same phase, cycle after cycle, are likely hormonal. Symptoms spread evenly across your cycle, or that follow your stress level, more likely have another cause, like stress or sleep. They can also be something to check with a doctor.

    Your data

    Everything is stored only in this browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded. The only thing sent anywhere is the search terms you use on the research page, which go to Europe PMC, a free public database of medical papers. Export regularly to keep a backup.

    Cycle Notes is a personal tracking tool, not a medical device. It can't diagnose anything. Use it to notice patterns and to have better conversations with a clinician.
